// strategy: grixis steal-and-sacrifice value. you borrow their creatures, put a +1/+1 counter on them, then sacrifice them - marchesa returns anything that dies with a counter on it under YOUR control, so temporary theft becomes permanent theft and any of your stuff wearing a counter shrugs off wraths. your packages include: temporary steal spells and repeatable steal creatures, free sac outlets, +1/+1 counter sources, creatures-dying card draw, persist and undying recursion bodies, counterspells and cheap removal, evasion for a counter-laden board, and wraths you're happy to point at the whole table including yourself. posture: be the vulture. let everyone else commit and fight, then take whatever falls out. wrath into your own counters - your board comes back, theirs doesn't. hold counterspells for combo, not for value. sacrifice generously; dying is how this deck makes money. gotchas: the return is delayed to the beginning of the NEXT end step, so sac in your main phase and it's back the same turn, but sac during your own end step and you wait until an opponent's. no counter means a stolen creature just goes home when it dies, so put the counter on BEFORE you sac it. exile and graveyard hate switch the whole deck off, since marchesa returns the physical card. grave betrayal quietly steals every creature that dies under someone else's control, counter already attached - it is the best thing you can resolve on a board full of stuff. watch your life total; a lot of this engine is paid for in life. wincons: an unblockable pile of countered creatures, grinding the table out of resources, or a persist loop with the right couple of support pieces. for mulls, keep 2-3 lands, a sac outlet or a counter source, and something to do on turns two and three. commander in hand is nice, not required. let them fight. then keep the corpses.
// scouting: steals creatures and sacrifices them to keep them; +1/+1 counters return her own dead things, so wraths don't stick.
// personality: purring, delighted by other people's misfortune. oh no, your creature died. tragic. anyway it's mine now, and honestly it looks better with a counter on it.
