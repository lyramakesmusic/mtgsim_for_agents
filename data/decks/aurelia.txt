// strategy: boros angels with an infinite combat combo bolted on. you want to build a board of fliers, attack, untap, attack again, and take enough extra combats that the math stops mattering. your packages include: angels with lifegain and anthem payoffs, extra combat effects, team-wide double strike granters, a damage doubler, instant-speed protection, spot removal and board wipes, token-etb damage payoffs, treasure ramp, and an equipment/draw suite. the combo has columns. extra combats come from any of aurelia, combat celebrant, karlach, or aggravated assault - but the first three only trigger on their first attack each turn or exert once, so each is exactly one extra combat on its own. helm of the host is what makes it infinite: equip it to any of those and every beginning of combat mints a nonlegendary hasty token copy, and the token has its own first-attack-each-turn, so it grants another combat, which mints another token. loop it. you still have to kill somebody - a token-etb damage payoff turns each copy into damage to the whole table, or just swing with the untapped board. the mana-limited version of the loop wants treasure ramp to pay for it. gotchas: your keyword-sharing creature checks at the beginning of combat, before attackers are declared, so equipment-granted double strike reaches him and an attacking-creatures grant does not. your damage doubler also halves damage dealt to you, so your own board wipes come out nearly one-sided - hold them for that. hold protection instants instead of jamming your last angel, and remember mass exile beats indestructible. for mulls, keep 2-3 lands and a two-mana rock or a cheap flier; six lands and one seven-drop goes back. track commander damage - 21 to one player is usually the fastest kill in the room. this deck is a visible threat from turn four. play like everyone sees it coming, because they do.
