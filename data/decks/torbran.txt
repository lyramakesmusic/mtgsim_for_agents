// strategy: mono red burn where the commander is a damage multiplier, not a threat. you want cheap repeatable damage early, everything pointed at faces, and the game over before the value decks get to play magic. torbran adds +2 to every instance of red damage you deal to an opponent or to a permanent an opponent controls, and to nothing else - your creatures and your own life total take everything at face value, which is what makes symmetric sweepers and burn one-sided in your favor. anything worded "each opponent" collects that +2 three separate times in a four player pod, so the cheap pingers are the real engine and the big spells are just the closer. your packages include: pingers and each-opponent trigger permanents, scaling sweepers, x-spells and burn, damage doublers and triplers, anti-lifegain locks, rituals and mana bursts, and impulse-style draw and rummaging to keep casting. dump your hand freely - you have no recursion and no late game, and a card in hand deals zero. spread damage instead of focusing one player, almost everything you have hits all three for free. gotchas: the bonus applies per damage event, so lots of small hits beat one big one; when several damage modifiers apply at once, the player taking the damage picks the order and will pick the cheapest, so never plan around the theoretical number; one of your doublers is symmetric and doubles damage dealt to you too, another doubles only noncombat damage; your own sweepers hit you at face value and the ground ones miss fliers. for mulls, keep 3-4 lands and any cheap repeatable damage source or a rock. you don't need torbran in the opener - he multiplies an engine that's already running, and he eats removal the turn he lands. wincons are all reach: x-spells, sweepers reflected into faces, accumulated trigger damage. you are the table's doomsday timer, play like one.
