// strategy: 1) Make a lot of mana — land drops before optional draw engines (commander tax is real and creature mana dies to wipes), dorks next, then Cryptolith Rite / Citanul Hierophants turn every squirrel into a Forest. Song of Freyalise is the same effect on a timer: chapters I-II give you a two-turn window to go infinite (all you need), and chapter III crowns the swing — a +1/+1 counter on everything plus vigilance, trample, indestructible. Cast it the turn before you crank. Vitalize is a one-mana ritual once creatures tap for mana, and Staff of Domination + Karametra's Acolyte (devotion 5+) is infinite mana that draws your whole deck — jackpot line, no tutor for the Staff. 2) Turn that mana into squirrels. 3) Turn those squirrels into pain. Combo lines: Squirrel Girl + Concordant Crossroads + (Rite or Hierophants or Freyalise) = infinite squirrels and mana — new tokens need haste to tap, so Crossroads sustains the loop. Crashing Drawbridge does not: its tap only blesses creatures already on board, once per turn — final swing or an early hasty commander only. Squirrel Girl + Supportive Parents also goes infinite — tapping two creatures is a cost, so it works through summoning sickness. Ivy Lane Denizen goes infinite with either Scurry Oak or Herd Baloth, no commander needed (each green creature ETB puts a counter on Oak/Baloth, which makes another token). Lightning Greaves haste the commander and shroud her through the announce window — but shroud blocks YOUR targeting too: no Overprotect, no Season of Growth, no Denizen counters on the wearer, so boots on Girl, counters at Baloth. Champion of Lambholt makes the swarm unblockable; Skullclamp, Bestiary and Toski keep cards flowing without overextending into wipes. Evolutionary Leap is wipe insurance: sacrifice the board in response and turn tokens into creature cards — die on your terms. Hold up protection — Overprotect, Collective Resistance, Druid's Deliverance, Fog, Spore Frog — and spend it on removal aimed at your final mana or haste enabler, never on earlier value exchanges; the table WILL have answers. Don't activate Squirrel Girl as a mana engine unless haste is already in play or the board survives a full rotation — Hierophants alone is not a loop. Kill on the turn you crank, not the turn after.
