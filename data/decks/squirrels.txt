// strategy: 1) Make a lot of mana — dorks first, then Cryptolith Rite / Citanul Hierophants turn every squirrel into a Forest. Song of Freyalise is the same effect on a timer: chapters I-II give you a two-turn window to go infinite (all you need), and chapter III crowns the swing — a +1/+1 counter on everything (permanent mana dorks under Yanggu) plus vigilance, trample, indestructible. Cast it the turn before you crank. Jiang Yanggu pairs specifically with Ivy Lane Denizen: every green creature that enters gets a counter from the Denizen, and anything with a counter is a mana dork under Yanggu — each new squirrel pays for the next. 2) Turn that mana into squirrels. 3) Turn those squirrels into pain. Combo lines: Squirrel Girl + Concordant Crossroads + (Rite or Hierophants or Freyalise) = infinite squirrels and mana — new tokens need haste to tap, so Crossroads sustains the loop. Crashing Drawbridge does not: its tap only blesses creatures already on board, once per turn — use it for the final swing after the crank, or an early hasty commander, not to run the loop. Squirrel Girl + Supportive Parents also goes infinite — tapping two creatures is a cost, so it works through summoning sickness. Scurry Oak + Ivy Lane Denizen loops squirrels with no commander needed (each green creature ETB puts a counter on Oak, which makes another squirrel). Champion of Lambholt makes the swarm unblockable; Skullclamp, Bestiary, Garruk's Uprising and Toski keep cards flowing without overextending into wipes. Hold up protection for the go-off turn — Overprotect, Collective Resistance, Druid's Deliverance, Fog, Spore Frog — the table WILL have answers, and the deck dies to one board wipe or a sac-outlet ping-off if you tap out and pray. Kill on the turn you crank, not the turn after.
