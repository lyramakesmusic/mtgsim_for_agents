// strategy: Make mana, make squirrels, find your combos. Get Squirrel Girl out asap. The combo pieces all have backups: haste comes from any of Concordant Crossroads, Shang-Chi, Thousand-Year Elixir (ability-haste counts — the tokens only need to tap), or Lightning Greaves (equip {0}: walk the boots down the line, each token taps while wearing them); creatures-tap-for-mana comes from any of Cryptolith Rite, Citanul Hierophants, Jaheira, or Song of Freyalise (chapters I-II). Squirrel Girl + any one from each column = infinite squirrels and mana. Two more combos if you can't find those: Squirrel Girl + Supportive Parents = infinite through summoning sickness (tapping two creatures is a cost), and Ivy Lane Denizen + (Scurry Oak or Herd Baloth) = infinite tokens, no commander needed. Staff of Domination + Karametra's Acolyte (devotion 5+) = infinite mana. Good lines: creature tutors (Evolutionary Leap, Time of Need, Traverse the Ulvenwald) can find combo pieces — Shang-Chi, Jaheira, Oak, Baloth, Acolyte. The fight spells (Prey Upon, Bushwhack, Prizefight, Bite Down) are janky removal: Squirrel Girl can fight a 3/3 with a problematic ability and win. Payoffs: Altar of the Brood mills the table per token, Throne of the God-Pharaoh drains for your tapped creatures (they tap for mana anyway), Prosperous Innkeeper gains life per body, Champion of Lambholt makes the swarm unblockable. Skullclamp, Bestiary and Toski keep cards flowing; Overprotect, Collective Resistance, Druid's Deliverance, Fog and Spore Frog protect the crank — the table WILL have answers. Kill on the turn you crank if you have haste — why wait. Without it, you have squirrels anyways.
