// strategy: 1) Make a lot of mana — dorks, then Cryptolith Rite / Citanul Hierophants / Song of Freyalise (chapters I-II are a two-turn mana window) turn every squirrel into a Forest. 2) Turn that mana into squirrels. 3) Turn those squirrels into pain. Lines worth knowing: Squirrel Girl + (Concordant Crossroads or Shang-Chi's ability-haste) + any tokens-tap-for-mana effect goes infinite. Squirrel Girl + Supportive Parents goes infinite through summoning sickness — tapping two creatures is a cost. Ivy Lane Denizen + Scurry Oak or Herd Baloth loops tokens with no commander involved. Staff of Domination + Karametra's Acolyte at devotion 5+ is infinite mana. Vitalize is a ritual once creatures tap for mana; Crashing Drawbridge and Lightning Greaves are haste; Evolutionary Leap is skullclamp-style creature tutoring for whatever piece is missing; Champion of Lambholt makes the swarm unblockable; Skullclamp, Bestiary and Toski keep the cards flowing. Overprotect, Collective Resistance, Druid's Deliverance, Fog and Spore Frog are protection — the table WILL have answers. Kill on the turn you crank, not the turn after.
