// strategy: Ramp into Squirrel Girl, then find the pieces that make her activation pay for itself. Her {1}{G}{G}{G} makes a squirrel for every squirrel you already control, so the board doubles per crank and the only question is mana. Two columns, each with backups: creatures-tap-for-mana comes from any of Cryptolith Rite, Citanul Hierophants, or Jaheira (Jaheira grants it to tokens only, all you need) — all continuous grants, so squirrels made mid-crank tap too. Haste comes from any of Concordant Crossroads, Crashing Drawbridge, Thousand-Year Elixir, or Shang-Chi. Squirrel Girl plus one from each column is infinite squirrels and infinite mana. Know which haste you have: Elixir and Shang-Chi only let you activate abilities as though creatures had haste, enough to tap for mana, not enough to attack — with those you win through Altar of the Brood, which mills each opponent per permanent entering, or Throne of the God-Pharaoh, which drains for your tapped creatures — you tapped them all for mana anyway. Crossroads and Drawbridge give real haste, so you can kill the turn you crank. Two more lines if the columns don't show: Squirrel Girl + Supportive Parents goes infinite once you have eight untapped squirrels, and it works through summoning sickness because tapping two creatures is a cost, not a tap ability; Ivy Lane Denizen + Scurry Oak is infinite tokens with no commander at all, since every green creature entering puts a counter on the Oak and every counter on the Oak makes another squirrel. Forgotten Ancient can feed the Oak too. Dig with Lead the Stampede, Realmwalker, and Thornvault Forager; keep cards flowing with Skullclamp and the creature-count draw spells. Second Harvest is a one-shot double of everything. Champion of Lambholt makes the swarm unblockable, Overrun and End-Raze Forerunners close. Hold Overprotect, Collective Resistance, Inspiring Call, or Fog — the table gets one shot at wrathing you. Play quiet until the crank starts.
