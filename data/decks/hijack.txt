// strategy: A voltron deck wearing a steal-denial mask. The kill is usually pump-math commander damage — Loki through the Access Tunnel, or a borrowed body doubled and thrown — and the theft suite is the mask: removal, defense, and the thing opponents play around while the 3/3 clocks them. Loki turns every spell that targets only a single creature into a theft (on your turn it untaps and hastes too), so aim the cheap tricks at THEIR creatures: the pump is a Threaten with the buff attached. Jeska's 0 makes a body deal triple combat damage — Loki plus any pump is 21 commander damage in one connect. Sac outlets (Bombardment, Barrage, Munitions, Ashnod's, Thermopod, Weaponize the Monsters, Bloodshot Cyclops) make thefts permanent; Fling, Thud and Soul's Fire convert power to face. Krark can't really lose in this deck: win the flip and the trick copies, lose and the steal already happened at cast — the spell just comes home for another theft later. Storm-Kiln Artist mints a Treasure per spell and Magda one per crime (targeting their stuff is a crime), so the tricks fund themselves. Pyromancer's Goggles doubles a trick cast with its mana — but copies aren't cast, so no extra Loki trigger; only true theft spells (Hijack, Act of Treason, Kari Zev's, Grab the Reins) double-grab. Isochron Scepter copies ARE cast: an imprinted trick steals every single turn. Fists of Flame plus the group-draw pieces (Howling Mine, Temple Bell, Snort) is a giant trampler from an empty board; Reliquary Tower keeps the ammo. Sticky Fingers on their creature is an aura-theft that leaves a Treasure engine; Caldera Pyremaw grows with every trick and throws its weight around; Spectacular Showdown goads the table's fatties into each other. Humble Defector loops: tap for two cards, it defects, steal it back with any trick tomorrow. Access Tunnel is tuned to Loki's 3 power; Rogue's Passage sends anything through unblocked.
