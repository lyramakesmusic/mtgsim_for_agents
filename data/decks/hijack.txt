// strategy: A voltron deck wearing a steal-denial mask. The kill is usually pump-math commander damage — Loki through the tunnel, or any borrowed body doubled twice — and the theft suite is the mask: removal, defense, and the thing opponents play around while the 3/3 clocks them. Loki turns every spell that targets only a single creature into a theft — on your turn it untaps and hastes too — so aim the one-mana tricks at THEIR creatures. The kill is burst math on a borrowed body: Bulk Up doubles its power, Assault Strobe / Temur Battle Rage / Team Tactics give double strike, Dictate of the Twin Gods doubles all of it again, and Thud or Fling adds its power once more after combat — a stolen 8/8 is ~90 damage across three cheap spells. Rionya copies the stolen creature at combat, X = tricks cast this turn, hasted, exiled at end step so the copies never go home; Raphael gives all attackers double strike. Sac outlets (Bombardment, Barrage, Munitions, Ashnod's, Thermopod, Weaponize the Monsters, Bloodshot Cyclops) make any theft permanent; Meekstone does it without an outlet — stolen 3+ power creatures go home tapped and stay that way. Humble Defector loops: tap for two cards, it defects, any trick steals it back next turn for two more. Pyromancer's Goggles doubles any trick cast with its mana — a copied Bulk Up is x4 power, a copied Thud flings the same body's power twice (copies don't pay the sacrifice), a copied Dragon Fodder is four goblins for the clamp, and a copied Hijack / Kari Zev's / Grab the Reins takes a second creature — but note copies aren't cast, so a copied trick does NOT trigger Loki; only the true theft spells double-grab. Isochron Scepter imprints a trick for repeatable theft; Gut Shot steals for zero via phyrexian; Flame Jab retraces; Seething Anger has buyback; Past in Flames rebuys whole turns. The table learns to hold mana open in fear of instant-speed theft — Power Surge burns them for exactly that. Magus of the Moon hijacks greedy manabases. Access Tunnel is tuned to Loki's 3 power and Rogue's Passage sends any borrowed body through unblocked. Against creature-light boards (stax, control) the tricks point at Loki himself — Bulk Up plus a double-strike effect is 21 commander damage in two swings; the steal deck carries a voltron mode in the command zone. Cantrip tricks (Ancestral Anger, Expedite, Stun, Might of the Meek, Renegade Tactics, Titan's Strength, Blazing Crescendo) replace themselves; Krenko + Skullclamp is the draw engine: goblins double each tap and every goblin is two cards — twenty cards deep finds the whole voltron package. Young Pyromancer and Dragon Fodder feed the same clamp and the altars once the table stops playing creatures out of fear — which is also a win.
