// strategy: aura voltron on a commander the table mostly cannot answer. she flies, she's hexproof, and opponents' spells and abilities can't make you sacrifice, so targeted removal and edicts are both dead against you - what's left is sweepers, chump blocks and mass exile. you want her down on turn four, an enchantress or two behind her, and 21 commander damage on one player fast. the sim doesn't track commander damage, so keep a note per opponent - that is the entire wincon. your packages include: mana dorks and cheap ramp, enchantress draw engines, auras that scale off how many enchantments you control, cheap keyword auras for trample, double strike, lifelink and evasion, a protection layer of aura armor, indestructible grants and instant-speed blankets, aura tutors and aura recursion, and a normal white removal suite. suit her up and swing; every aura draws you into the next one, so you don't run dry. gotchas: some enchantresses trigger on casting the aura and some on it entering, so a countered aura still draws off half of them. aura armor is one-shot - the aura dies instead of the creature, which also shrinks every count-your-enchantments buff she's wearing. winds of rath spares enchanted creatures, so it is a one-sided sweeper you fire off into your own board. daybreak coronet only attaches to a creature that already has another aura on it. every aura is a card, so if she ever gets exiled you lose the whole stack at once - put on exactly enough to kill and hold the rest in hand. hold interaction for the sweeper and mass-exile decks; kill those players first. for mulls, keep 2-3 lands plus a dork or a ramp spell and at least one cheap aura or an enchantress; fat auras with no acceleration do nothing. wincon: commander damage, and only that. protect her like the game depends on her, because it does.
