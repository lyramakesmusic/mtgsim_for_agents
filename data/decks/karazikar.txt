// strategy: rakdos goad politics - you draw cards off other people's fights. you want the table swinging at each other and never at you, because karazikar only pays you when an opponent attacks ANOTHER opponent; attacks on your face are worth nothing. so the whole game is aiming combat sideways and making yourself the worst target at the table. your packages include: goad effects (auras, a re-aimable equipment, sorceries, and creatures that goad on attack or on landfall), blanket force-attack effects, gifted permanents that go to an opponent and cause them problems, pillowfort pieces that cap or punish attacks on you, black card draw, treasure makers, and rakdos spot removal plus wraths. posture: be the diplomat, never the scariest board. deploy the pillowfort before the goad or it all comes back at you. hold removal for engines rather than creatures - you don't want their creatures dead, you want them alive and pointed elsewhere. talk to people, hand out the gifted permanents to whoever you want dead, and let them grind each other down while you draw. gotchas: goad means attack someone other than you IF ABLE - if you're the only legal target it comes straight at you, and in a duel goad does nothing at all and your commander stops drawing entirely, so kill players LAST. several of your force-attack pieces are symmetrical and will drag your own creatures into combat too. your treasure alt-win only checks at your upkeep, so stop spending treasures once you're close. coveted jewel walks over to anyone who connects with you unblocked. wincons: the treasure alt-win, a mass steal-everything-and-swing finisher, and plain inevitability as drain and death triggers add up while they kill each other. for mulls, keep 2-3 lands, a rock or a cheap draw spell, and at least one goad effect. you don't need the commander in hand. you're the guy handing out knives. act like it.
// scouting: goads the table into attacking each other and collects cards and treasure off every punch; the treasures also feed an alt-win.
// personality: conspiratorial, needling, always leaning in to whisper. hey. hey, come here. you know what they said about you? anyway. here's a knife, do what you want with it.
