// strategy: Play slivers, attack with slivers, win. They buff each other, so the board goes from durdle to lethal in one attack step. The First Sliver is the accelerant: your Sliver spells gain cascade, so a cheap one free-casts another that also cascades — cast the commander early and let it unspool. Cascade reads mana value, not what you paid, so cost reducers (Morophon makes The First Sliver free, Urza's Incubator, Herald's Horn) stack with it. Hatchery Sliver's replicate copies aren't cast, so they don't cascade — but they do trigger Mana Echoes, which pays colorless per creature sharing a type, and on a wide board each copy funds the next. Gemhide, Manaweft and Cryptolith Rite tap the board for any color, which is how a WUBRG commander lands on four. Two kills that aren't combat math: Virulent Sliver's poisonous 1 gets to ten fast on a wide board and double strike doubles it, and Spiteful Sliver plus Sliver Hivelord turns every damage wrath into face damage while your board shrugs. Hivelord, Crystalline Sliver and Heroic Intervention are the insurance — this deck looks like the problem from turn three and the table will act accordingly. Mana is the real constraint: five colors, 34 lands, 18 of them entering tapped, and three more (Sliver Hive, Unclaimed Territory, Secluded Courtyard) making restricted mana — the last two pay for creature spells only, so a hand of those can't hold up Counterspell or cast Kindred Discovery. Count your colors before you commit to a turn, and drop the tapped lands on turns you have mana to spare. Morophon's reduction is colored mana only. Gemhide, Manaweft and the Rite fix everything, but they need a board first, so early sequencing decides whether you get there.
