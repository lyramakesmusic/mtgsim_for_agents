// strategy: Tribal aggro that wins by arithmetic — every Sliver makes every other Sliver bigger, so the board reads as durdle right up until it's lethal in one attack step. The First Sliver is the accelerant: your Sliver spells gain cascade, so a cheap Sliver free-casts another that cascades too, and one two-drop can unspool half the deck. Cascade reads mana value, not what you paid, so the reducers stack with it instead of shrinking the chains — Morophon naming Sliver makes The First Sliver free (colored mana only, a 2R Sliver still costs {2}), Incubator takes {2} off, Herald's Horn {1}. Hatchery Sliver's replicate copies are copies, not casts, so they don't cascade, but they're still creatures entering and Mana Echoes pays colorless for every creature sharing a type, so on a wide board each copy funds the next. The mana is the soft spot: five colors, 34 lands, 18 of them entering tapped, and Sliver Hive, Unclaimed Territory and Secluded Courtyard make restricted mana — the last two buy creature spells only, so a hand of those can't hold up Counterspell or cast Kindred Discovery. Gemhide, Manaweft and Cryptolith Rite fix everything, but they need a board first, which makes the first four turns a sequencing puzzle: the tapped lands go down on the turns with mana to spare. Keeps live or die on the manabase: you want lands and all five colors, one short at worst, because the deck can't fix its way out of a two-land hand — the fixers are creatures that need casting first. A spell-dense seven is a mulligan. Virulent Sliver is a second wincon hiding in a one-drop — poisonous 1 across a wide board reaches ten counters well before anyone hits zero life, and double strike from Bonescythe or Thrumming Hivepool doubles the count. Spiteful Sliver plus Sliver Hivelord turns every damage-based wrath into face damage while the board shrugs it off. Hivelord, Crystalline Sliver and Heroic Intervention are the insurance, and the deck needs them: it reads as the table's problem from turn three.
