// strategy: Graveyard value engine, now with teeth. The yard is your second hand: sac cheap bodies for value (Skullclamp, altars, Village Rites, Yawgmoth), grow Meren's experience, rebuy threats every end step. Edicts tax the table's best creatures. Entomb/Buried Alive put Mikaeus, the Unhallowed in the yard early — Meren or Reanimate brings him back, and Mikaeus + Triskelion is a game-ending two-card combo (infinite damage via undying loops; demonstrate it and claim the win). Toxic Deluge and Grave Titan close grindy games. Board wipes barely hurt you — everyone rebuilds, you reanimate.
