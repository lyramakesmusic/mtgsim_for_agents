// strategy: a golgari sacrifice engine that plays out of the graveyard, not your hand. cheap disposable bodies, a sac outlet, death payoffs, and meren rebuys a creature at your end step. the combo: mikaeus, the unhallowed + triskelion + any free sac outlet - remove triskelion's counters, sac it, undying hands it a fresh one, repeat. free outlets come from any of viscera seer, carrion feeder, ashnod's altar, phyrexian altar, altar of dementia or yawgmoth. routes to the pieces: entomb and buried alive put them in the yard on purpose, stitcher's supplier, satyr wayfinder and grisly salvage by accident; reanimate, animate dead, victimize, living death and meren herself bring them back, and victimize alone puts both onto the battlefield at once. second combo: yawgmoth + two undying bodies (young wolf, butcher ghoul, or anything non-human while mikaeus is out) + blood artist or zulaport drains the table, because the -1/-1 counter cancels the undying +1/+1 counter each pass. gotchas: experience counters are yours, not hers, and survive her death - chumping or trading her costs only the tax. her end step return hits the battlefield only if the creature's mana value is at or under your experience, else it goes to hand, and both combo pieces cost six. tokens give experience but can't be rebought. midnight reaper and grim haruspex only see nontoken deaths; ayara only counts black creatures. sacrificing is a cost, not a target: sac in response to exile and keep the trigger. caustic caterpillar is your repeatable answer to graveyard hate since meren rebuys it, and spore frog plus meren is a fog every turn. weaknesses: graveyard exile, mass exile, speed. for mulls, keep 2-4 lands plus a cheap body or an outlet; a hand of six-drops is a mulligan. wincons: the combo, or grinding on drain triggers, edicts and recursive fatties. be the patient value deck; don't look like the threat until you can close.
